# The 60-Day Leadership Academy — Map

**Principle:** you can't lead others until you lead yourself; you can't lead at work in a way you don't also live at home.
Each day = one idea from a great book or leader → practiced at work **and** at home → one small action within 24h.

## The 10 competencies (rate yourself 1–10 on Day 0, 30 and 60)

1. **Self-leadership** — self-awareness, emotional control, ownership
2. **Vision & purpose** — knowing and communicating the "why"
3. **Trust** — character + competence, keeping promises, psychological safety
4. **Communication** — listening, hard conversations, influence
5. **Feedback** — giving and receiving it, accountability
6. **Developing people** — motivation, delegation, coaching
7. **Execution** — priorities, decisions, rhythms
8. **Conflict & resilience** — healthy disagreement, crisis, setbacks
9. **Culture** — rituals, belonging, leading change
10. **Character & legacy** — humility, service, what you're building over a lifetime

| 3 | Trust & Safety | 13–18 | Lencioni *Five Dysfunctions*, Brené Brown *Dare to Lead*, Edmondson *The Fearless Organization*, Covey Jr. *Speed of Trust*, Sinek *Leaders Eat Last* |
| 4 | Communication & Listening | 19–24 | Carnegie, Patterson et al. *Crucial Conversations*, Voss *Never Split the Difference*, Rosenberg *Nonviolent Communication*, Bungay Stanier *The Coaching Habit* |
| 5 | Feedback & Accountability | 25–30 | Kim Scott *Radical Candor*, CCL's SBI model, Stone & Heen *Thanks for the Feedback*, Marquet *Turn the Ship Around*, Dweck *Mindset* |
| 6 | Developing People | 31–36 | Pink *Drive*, Wiseman *Multipliers*, Hersey–Blanchard Situational Leadership, Grove *High Output Management*, *Trillion Dollar Coach* (Bill Campbell) |
| 7 | Decisions & Execution | 37–42 | Eisenhower, McKeown *Essentialism*, Bezos shareholder letters, Dalio *Principles*, *The 4 Disciplines of Execution*, Lencioni *Death by Meeting*, Feiler *Secrets of Happy Families* |
| 8 | Conflict, Pressure & Resilience | 43–48 | Goodwin *Team of Rivals*, Fisher & Ury *Getting to Yes*, Gottman, Shackleton, Churchill, Holiday *The Obstacle Is the Way* |
| 9 | Culture & Change | 49–54 | Coyle *The Culture Code*, Collins *Good to Great*, Greenleaf servant leadership, Mandela, Kotter *Leading Change*, Heath *Switch* |
| 10 | Legacy & Integration | 55–60 | Christensen *How Will You Measure Your Life?*, Arbinger *Leadership and Self-Deception*, Frankl, Kouzes & Posner *The Leadership Challenge* |

Day 6 of every module = **review & role-play day**. Day 30 = midpoint re-assessment. Day 60 = capstone: write your personal leadership credo.
